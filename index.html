Building Community

Wellness in Da Park is Philadelphia’s annual free event that was created to help our community grow together in health knowledge and education on plant based foods. It showcases local community talent as well as host inspirational guests from other cities.

Wellness in Da Park is sponsored by CEO Mika’il Abdul-Karim of Diverse Body Sculpturing Inc. As an international personal trainer, student of biomechanics with experience in massage therapy, Mika'il provides a different level of personal fitness to his clients.

Wellness in Da Park, inspired by Mika’il, is a family fun filled event that has activities for everyone: lectures, healthy foods, live entertainment, vendors, etc. as well as products and rides for the children. Moon bouncing, face painting, horseback riding, and clown are some of the activities just for the kids.

Together, the Wellness in Da Park team and celebrities will be working to further the cause with on-site free health screenings along with a men’s and women’s boot camp.
